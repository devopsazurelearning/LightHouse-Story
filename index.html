The Lighthouse Keeper

A short story about an old lighthose and a promise.

Chapter 1: The Last Light

Every evening at dusk, Mara climbed the ninety-two steps to the top of the old lighthouse and lit the lamp. The village below called it habit. Mara called it a promise.

Tonight the sea was quiet, too quiet, and the horizon held one small light that did not belong to any ship she knew.
