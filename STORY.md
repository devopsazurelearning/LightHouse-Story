## Chapter 2: The Stranger's Boat

At first light, a small wooden boat drifted against the rocks below the lighthouse. Nobody was rowing it. Inside lay a brass lantern, still warm, and a folded map with Mara's name written across the top.

She carried the lantern up the ninety-two steps, one at a time, and set it beside her own lamp. The two flames leaned toward each other as if they were old friends.

## Chapter 3: The Promise

By noon the map had led her to a hidden door in the base of the tower. Behind it was a narrow room full of letters, each one addressed to a ship that never came home.

Mara read them all before sunset. Then she lit the lamp, brighter than she ever had, so that every lost ship would know there was still a harbour waiting, and that someone had kept the promise.
